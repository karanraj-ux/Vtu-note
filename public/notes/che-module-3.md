## 1. Conducting Polymers

Conventional polymers are electrical insulators because their valence electrons are tightly localized in covalent sigma ($\sigma$) bonds. Conducting polymers possess conjugated double-single bond alternating sequences along their carbon backbones ($\pi$-electron delocalization).

### Mechanism of Conduction in Polyaniline (PANI):
- **Doping**: Electrical conductivity can be increased by 10 to 12 orders of magnitude via chemical or electrochemical doping.
- **p-Doping (Oxidative)**: Removal of $\pi$-electrons using oxidizing agents ($\text{I}_2, \text{FeCl}_3, \text{HCl}$) creates positive polarons and bipolarons (radical cations).
- **Applications**:
  1. Anti-static coatings on computer casings and cleanroom packaging.
  2. Flexible printed electronics and OLED (Organic Light Emitting Diode) displays.
  3. Electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding.

## 2. Organic Light Emitting Diodes (OLEDs)

An OLED is a solid-state semiconductor display device consisting of a stack of thin organic molecular or polymeric films sandwiched between two electrical contacts (anode and cathode).

### Architecture & Layer Function:
1. **Substrate**: Transparent glass or flexible polymer film (PET).
2. **Anode**: Indium Tin Oxide (ITO) — transparent conductive oxide with high work function.
3. **Hole Transport Layer (HTL)**: TPD or NPB ($N,N'$-bis(naphthalen-1-yl)).
4. **Emissive Layer (EML)**: $\text{Alq}_3$ (Tris(8-hydroxyquinolinato)aluminium) or phosphorescent iridium complexes.
5. **Electron Transport Layer (ETL)**: $\text{Alq}_3$, bathocuproine (BCP).
6. **Cathode**: Low work function metal alloy (LiF/Al or Mg:Ag).

## 3. Carbon Nanomaterials: Graphene & Carbon Nanotubes (CNTs)

### Graphene:
- Single atom thick two-dimensional sheet of $sp^2$-hybridized carbon arranged in a hexagonal honeycomb crystal lattice.
- **Properties**:
  - Electrical conductivity: Electron mobility $> 200,000\text{ cm}^2/(\text{V}\cdot\text{s})$.
  - Tensile strength: $\approx 130\text{ GPa}$ (100 times stronger than steel of equivalent thickness).
  - Thermal conductivity: $5000\text{ W}/(\text{m}\cdot\text{K})$.

### Carbon Nanotubes (CNTs):
- Cylindrical rolled-up graphene tubes:
  - **SWCNTs (Single-Walled Carbon Nanotubes)**: Diameter $0.7 - 2\text{ nm}$.
  - **MWCNTs (Multi-Walled Carbon Nanotubes)**: Concentric cylinders with inter-wall spacing of $0.34\text{ nm}$.
- **Synthesis by Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD)**: Hydrocarbon gases ($\text{CH}_4, \text{C}_2\text{H}_2$) decomposed over transition metal catalyst nanoparticles (Fe, Co, Ni) at $700 - 900^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Applications in Computing**: Ultra-fast ballistic nanoscale field effect transistors (CNFETs), high-density capacitor electrodes, and thermal interface heat spreaders for CPUs.
